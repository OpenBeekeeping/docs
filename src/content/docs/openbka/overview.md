OpenBKA helps beekeeping associations (BKAs) look after their members and records. It’s open source, and you can either host it yourself or use the hosted version at [openbka.org](https://openbka.org).

:::caution[Early development]
OpenBKA is not yet ready for use. This page describes what it is being built to do.
:::

## Signing in

Members sign in with their [Open Beekeeping account](/accounts/signing-in/). OpenBKA has no passwords or login forms of its own; it keeps each person’s account ID, email and name.

## Where your data lives

Member data is stored in Cloudflare’s EU jurisdiction, so it never leaves the EU. That suits UK and EU associations under (UK) GDPR.

## Hosted or self-hosted

- **Hosted:** [openbka.org](https://openbka.org) runs OpenBKA for you.
- **Self-hosted:** run your own copy on a free Cloudflare account. See [Self-hosting](/openbka/self-hosting/).
