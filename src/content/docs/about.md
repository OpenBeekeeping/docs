Open Beekeeping makes open source code and tools for beekeepers. Each tool is its own product with its own name.

| Tool                                 | What it’s for                                                    | Status            |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| [OpenBKA](/openbka/overview/)        | Running a beekeeping association: members, records and documents | Early development |
| OpenApiary                           | Keeping track of your own colonies and inspections               | Planned           |
| [Your account](/accounts/signing-in/) | One sign-in for every Open Beekeeping app                        | Early development |

## What “open” means here

- **The code is public.** Anyone can read it on [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenBeekeeping).
- **It’s free to use.** You can run the tools yourself, for your association or your own apiary, without paying anyone.
- **It’s open to change.** You can suggest improvements, or change your own copy to suit how you work.

Where we offer a hosted version, such as [openbka.org](https://openbka.org), it runs the same code you can host yourself.

## Your data

Each app keeps only the data it needs. Your account holds who you are (your name, email and the way you sign in); your association’s records stay with your association’s copy of OpenBKA.
